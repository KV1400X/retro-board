## Add to Home Screen (iPhone)

1. Open the live URL in **Safari** (not Chrome).
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Confirm name “Retro Board” → Add.
4. Launch from the home screen icon (standalone, no Safari chrome).

## Play vs Edit

| Mode | What happens |
|------|----------------|
| **Play** (default) | Tap pads to fire sounds. Multi-touch OK. Stop button kills all voices. |
| **Edit** | Tap a pad → rename, recolor, **Record** from mic, **Import** file, Preview, Clear, Save. |

Switch with the Play / Edit toggle in the top bar.

## First-run jam

1. Open the app → starter Click / Beep / Blip / Thud tones already on pads.
2. Switch to **Edit** → tap a pad → **Record** (allow mic) → Stop → **Save**.
3. Switch to **Play** → tap that pad — your recording plays.
4. Reload Safari / relaunch PWA — pad + audio still there (IndexedDB).
